---
title: Troubleshooting
description: "When the tunnel is up but requests aren't landing, walk this checklist."

---

Most "it doesn't work" reports turn out to be one of three things:

1. **The sidecar isn't running**, or is running with a stale `--local-port` flag.
2. **The local service isn't actually listening** on the port you bound.
3. **The local service itself is returning the error** (4xx, 5xx, rate-limited, etc.) — the tunnel is passing it through correctly.

See [Tunnel diagnostics](/docs/troubleshooting/tunnel-diagnostics) for the systematic walk.
